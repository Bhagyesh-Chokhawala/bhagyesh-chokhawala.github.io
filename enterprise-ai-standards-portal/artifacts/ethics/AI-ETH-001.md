# AI-ETH-001 — Human Impact and Ethical Risk Assessment

**Domain:** Ethics  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Architecture, Build & Evaluation, Deployment

## Standard

Medium- and high-risk AI use cases must assess affected users, potential harms, bias, contestability, accessibility, and human review requirements.

## Required controls

- Identify affected stakeholder groups
- Assess material harms and disparate impacts
- Define human review and appeal
- Evaluate accessibility and inclusion
- Document mitigation and residual risk

## Required evidence

- AI impact assessment
- Bias/fairness evaluation
- Human oversight design
- Residual-risk acceptance

- User Trust & Completeness
- Governance
