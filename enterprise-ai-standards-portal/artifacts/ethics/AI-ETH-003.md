# AI-ETH-003 — Human Oversight and Redress

**Domain:** Ethics  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Architecture, Implementation, Runtime

## Standard

High-impact AI decisions require defined human oversight, override, escalation, and redress pathways appropriate to the risk tier.

## Required controls

- Define when human approval is mandatory
- Provide user/operator override
- Define escalation and appeal paths
- Log human overrides and reasons
- Review recurring overrides for systemic issues

## Required evidence

- Human-in-the-loop workflow
- Override test
- Appeal/redress procedure
- Override analytics
