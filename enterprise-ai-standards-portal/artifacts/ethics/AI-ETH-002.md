# AI-ETH-002 — Transparency and AI Disclosure

**Domain:** Ethics  
**Requirement:** SHOULD  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Implementation, Deployment, Runtime

## Standard

Users should be informed when AI materially contributes to recommendations, decisions, generated responses, or automated actions.

## Required controls

- Disclose material AI participation
- Explain limitations where relevant
- Provide provenance or rationale for consequential outputs
- Avoid deceptive human-like representation

## Required evidence

- UI disclosure evidence
- Explanation sample
- User research or accessibility review

## Framework mapping

- Ethics
- User Trust & Completeness
