# AI-GOV-002 — Traffic-Light Compliance Decision

**Domain:** Governance  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R1, R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Build & Evaluation, Deployment

## Standard

Mandatory control failure results in Red/blocked status; Amber requires a documented, time-bound exception; Green requires all mandatory controls.

## Required controls

- Compute status from mandatory controls
- Block production on Red
- Require owner, expiry and compensating controls for Amber
- Document N/A rationale
- Retain release decision

## Required evidence

- Compliance report
- Exception record
- Release decision
