# AI-GOV-001 — AI Risk Classification

**Domain:** Governance  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R1, R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Architecture, Deployment

## Standard

Every AI use case must be assigned a risk tier based on impact, autonomy, data sensitivity, affected users, and consequences of failure before production release.

## Required controls

- Assess autonomy and impact
- Assess data sensitivity
- Assess user/operational consequences
- Assign R1-R4 tier
- Reassess after material scope or autonomy changes

## Required evidence

- Risk assessment
- Risk tier decision
- Reassessment trigger

## Framework mapping

- Governance
- Security
- Ethics
