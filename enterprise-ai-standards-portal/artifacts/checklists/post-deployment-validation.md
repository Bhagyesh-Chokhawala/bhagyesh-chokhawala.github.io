# Post-Deployment Validation Checklist

- [ ] Production quality matches approved evaluation baseline
- [ ] Retrieval quality and source freshness remain stable
- [ ] No unexpected sensitive-data exposure is detected
- [ ] Hallucination / unsupported-claim rate remains within threshold
- [ ] Tool calls remain within authorization boundaries
- [ ] Human override and escalation rates are reviewed
- [ ] Bias / fairness indicators are reviewed where applicable
- [ ] User feedback and usability signals are reviewed
- [ ] Business KPIs and task-completion metrics are reviewed
- [ ] Cost per successful outcome is reviewed
- [ ] Security and AI incidents are reviewed
- [ ] Continue / adjust / rollback decision is recorded
