# AI-ARCH-001 — Architectural Separation

**Domain:** Architecture  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R1, R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Architecture, Implementation

## Standard

Separate systems of engagement, AI intelligence/orchestration, controlled action layers, and systems of record. AI output alone is not authoritative enterprise state.

## Required controls

- Identify system of record and ownership
- Separate AI inference from authoritative state
- Enforce policy before state-changing execution
- Document transaction, audit, and failure boundaries

## Required evidence

- Approved architecture diagram
- System context/data-flow diagram
- System-of-record ownership record
- Failure and compensation design

## Framework mapping

- Technology Readiness
- Governance
- Security
