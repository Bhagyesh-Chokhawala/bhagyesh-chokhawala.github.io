# AI-DEP-003 — Risk-Based Production Deployment Gate

**Domain:** Deployment  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R1, R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Deployment

## Standard

Production promotion must require strategic fit, readiness, risk/control, security/ethics, production readiness, and post-launch value checkpoints appropriate to risk.

## Required controls

- Assign risk tier before promotion
- Block on failed mandatory controls
- Collect architecture/security/ethics/evaluation evidence
- Verify runbook and rollback
- Record release decision and exceptions

## Required evidence

- Gate decision record
- Risk tier record
- Evidence bundle
- Exception approvals

## Framework mapping

- Governance
- Security
- Ethics
- Business Value
