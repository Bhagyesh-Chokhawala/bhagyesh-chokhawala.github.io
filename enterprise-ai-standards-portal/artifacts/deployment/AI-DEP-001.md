# AI-DEP-001 — Environment Separation

**Domain:** Deployment  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R1, R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Deployment

## Standard

Production AI workloads, credentials, data access, and policy configurations must be separated from development, experimentation, and evaluation environments.

## Required controls

- Separate production credentials and secrets
- Prevent development access to production-only resources
- Use environment-specific configuration
- Control promotion between environments

## Required evidence

- Environment architecture
- Credential isolation test
- Promotion workflow

## Framework mapping

- Security
- Technology Readiness
- Operating Model
