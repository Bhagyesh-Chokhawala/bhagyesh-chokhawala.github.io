# AI-OPS-001 — Four-Plane AI Observability

**Domain:** Operations  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R1, R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Runtime

## Standard

Production AI must expose system, AI, business, and governance telemetry sufficient to detect quality, security, cost, and outcome degradation.

## Required controls

- Monitor system latency/errors/availability
- Monitor model, token, retrieval and tool behavior
- Monitor task and business outcomes
- Monitor policy, security and human-override events

## Required evidence

- Dashboard
- Alert configuration
- Telemetry schema
- Operational review record

## Framework mapping

- Technology Readiness
- Business Value
- Governance
