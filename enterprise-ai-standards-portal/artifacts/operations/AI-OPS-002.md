# AI-OPS-002 — End-to-End AI Traceability

**Domain:** Operations  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Runtime

## Standard

Production AI interactions must be traceable across correlation ID, identities, prompt/model version, retrieval, tool calls, policy decisions, approvals, and business result.

## Required controls

- Generate correlation identifier
- Record relevant component versions
- Trace retrieved sources and tool calls
- Trace policy and human approval decisions
- Protect sensitive prompt content according to privacy policy

- Trace sample
