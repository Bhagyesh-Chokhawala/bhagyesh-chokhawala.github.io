# AI-OPS-004 — AI-Specific Incident Response

**Domain:** Operations  
**Requirement:** MUST  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Runtime

## Standard

Operational processes must classify and respond to AI incidents such as harmful action, data disclosure, unauthorized action, systematic hallucination, bias, prompt injection, and provider degradation.

## Required controls

- Define AI incident categories and severities
- Define containment and rollback actions
- Preserve evidence for investigation
- Notify appropriate security/ethics/business owners
- Feed lessons into evaluation and policy updates

## Required evidence

- AI incident playbook
- Escalation matrix
- Post-incident review template
