# AI-OPS-005 — AI Service Level Objectives

**Domain:** Operations  
**Requirement:** SHOULD  
**Applicable risk tiers:** R1, R2, R3, R4  
**Lifecycle phases:** Runtime

## Standard

Production AI should define measurable thresholds for availability, latency, task completion, groundedness, unsafe outputs, tool failures, escalation, and cost.

## Required controls

- Define risk-appropriate SLOs
- Measure business and AI quality together
- Alert on threshold breach
- Review cost per successful task
- Link SLO breach to operational response

## Required evidence

- SLO document
- Dashboard
- Alert thresholds
- Monthly service review

## Framework mapping

- Technology Readiness
- Business Value
- User Trust & Completeness
