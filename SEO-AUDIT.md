# SEO audit summary

## Critical fixes

1. The Digital Transformation publication link used a lowercase directory that did
   not exist in the repository.
2. Its DOI was incorrectly duplicated from the Capability-Centric paper.
3. The sitemap did not comprehensively expose all core pages and publication
   landing pages.
4. The homepage did not provide complete profile-page structured data or social
   sharing metadata.
5. The CSCI paper was exposed only as a PDF, without an HTML landing page carrying
   scholarly metadata.

## Additional improvements

- Descriptive titles and snippets
- Consistent canonical URLs
- Open Graph and Twitter cards
- Person identity reconciliation through Google Scholar, ORCID, LinkedIn, and Medium
- Publication and blog item lists
- Breadcrumb data
- Dynamic year and accessible navigation
- 404 handling, RSS, favicon, manifest, and internal-link improvements
