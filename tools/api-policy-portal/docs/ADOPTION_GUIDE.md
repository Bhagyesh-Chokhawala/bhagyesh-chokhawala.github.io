# Cap & Grow Adoption

## Cap risk
Inventory the estate, freeze net-new anti-patterns, and introduce minimum spec and policy gates.

## Stabilize
Prioritize critical APIs and add specs, authorization, failure tests, observability, health, and integrity.

## Grow platform
Publish templates, scorecards, idempotency/outbox/reconciliation libraries, and MCP/A2A governance.

## Scale adoption
Migrate in waves, coach teams, and measure portfolio adherence.
