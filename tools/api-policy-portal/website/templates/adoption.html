Transition approach

Cap & Grow adoption

Adopt the standards incrementally while stopping new portfolio risk.

1

Cap risk

Inventory APIs, identify anti-patterns, freeze risky net-new endpoints, and establish minimum spec and security gates.

2

Stabilize

Add contracts, object authorization, failure-path tests, observability, and integrity evidence to critical APIs.

3

Grow platform

Publish templates, reusable evaluators, integrity libraries, MCP/A2A governance, and automated scorecards.

4

Scale adoption

Migrate in waves, coach teams, publish portfolio dashboards, and use risk-based remediation for legacy APIs.
