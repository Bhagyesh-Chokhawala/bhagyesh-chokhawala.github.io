# HDAE Governance Charter

## Principles
- Human purpose and accountability cannot be delegated.
- Agent authority must be explicit, bounded, revocable, and traceable.
- High-impact or irreversible actions require proportionate human control.
- Runtime policy enforcement is required for material actions.
- Applications retain domain invariants and authoritative state.
- Every material action must resolve to an agent identity, represented principal, and accountable human/organization.
- Recovery, intervention, and termination mechanisms are mandatory.

## Required owners
Business Outcome Owner; Enterprise Architect; AI Platform Owner; Security/IAM;
Risk/Compliance; Domain/Application Owner; Data Owner; Operations/SRE.
