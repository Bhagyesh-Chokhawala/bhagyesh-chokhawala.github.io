# BAR-HDAE Process Builder Guide

## Create any business capability

1. Define a process name, domain, description, target outcome, value owner, baseline, and affected stakeholders.
2. Complete the 12 toolkit artifacts. These capture the process-specific design; they are not a fixed operational workflow.
3. Provide evidence for G0 Business Legitimacy and G1 Architecture Readiness before building the implementation baseline.
4. Implement and test with restricted authority in development and test.
5. Run the G2 Controlled Pilot only after policy, trace, recovery, adoption, and KPI evidence is available.
6. Record G3 Enterprise Scale only after sustained value, readiness, responsible adoption, and continuous assurance are demonstrated.
7. Use runtime evidence to choose scale, hold, remediate, or stop. Do not mark a gate complete solely because a document exists.

## Seven HDAE phases and twelve artifacts

1. **Scope outcome episode:** Outcome Charter.
2. **Model Intent Envelope:** Enterprise Intent Canvas; Intent Envelope Schema.
3. **Map capabilities and contracts:** Capability-Readiness Map; Outcome Contract Template.
4. **Design delegation and autonomy:** Agent Authority Profile; Autonomy-Risk Matrix.
5. **Assemble agent toolchain:** Tool and Action Registry; Policy and Control Pack.
6. **Engineer integrity and evidence:** Transaction and Recovery Map; Traceability and Evaluation Schema.
7. **Validate, learn, and scale:** Governance, Incident, and Realization Playbook.

## Evidence bridge

For each material action, preserve links from intent through plan, principal/workload identity, policy decision/version, tool invocation, domain transaction, human intervention, postcondition, outcome, and benefit. Use the same correlation identifiers across runtime traces and BAR realization reviews.

## Measurement and maturity

Measure BAR-B value and benefit ownership; BAR-A data, dependency, resilience, and lifecycle readiness; HDAE Intent Coverage, Policy Mediation Rate, trace completeness, unauthorized actions, approval latency, intervention, compensation, and recovery time; and BAR-R adoption, trust, incidents, residual risk, and realized benefit. L0-L4 measures institutional capability, not model sophistication. The manuscript is conceptual and these measures are not empirically validated universal scales.

## Samples and implementation boundary

The customer-onboarding and subscription-to-cash samples prefill all artifacts for demonstration. Replace sample values and supply real evidence before pilot or scale decisions. Generated packages are implementation baselines: they do not provision infrastructure or connect live IAM, policy, API, observability, workflow, or change-management systems.
