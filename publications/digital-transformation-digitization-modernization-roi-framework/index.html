Abstract

Digital initiatives are frequently labeled as transformations even when their primary purpose is to convert analog information, replace legacy technology, or optimize an existing process. This paper distinguishes digitization, digital modernization, and digital transformation and links strategic intent, lifecycle cost, adoption-adjusted benefits, resilience, risk reduction, and post-implementation measurement.
