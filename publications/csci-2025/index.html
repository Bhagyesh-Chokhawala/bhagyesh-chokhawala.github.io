Cognitive Artificial Intelligence in Business Process Management: An Adaptive and Context-Aware Process Optimization Framework

Bhagyeshkumar Chokhawala and Atif Farid Mohammad

2025 International Conference on Computational Science and Computational Intelligence, Las Vegas, Nevada, United States.

Download Searchable PDF Google Scholar Profile

Overview

This paper introduces the Cognitive BPM Integration Framework, a layered architecture combining process mining, contextual reasoning, adaptive decision support, governed execution, and continuous feedback to improve business process adaptability, transparency, and accountability.

Keywords

Cognitive AI · Business Process Management · Process Mining · Adaptive Decision Support · Explainable AI · Governance
